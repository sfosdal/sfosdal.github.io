The Importance of Routine Subaru Service and What's Involved

Take care of your Subaru and it will take care of you. It’s pretty simple, but when it comes to what’s required that’s where things can get complicated and depending on who you call you may get wildly varying prices and vague answers as to what is included. More importantly, a common trick that many dealers use involves giving a quote that seems very affordable but without an explanation of what is done. Unfortunately, after dropping your Subaru off for said service you will often get a call presenting a menu of other items that aren’t included likely adding up to substantially more than the original quote. We keep it simple. We follow Subaru’s recommendations using genuine parts and fluids to keep your warranty intact and your Subaru on the road for years to come. Below you’ll find descriptions of the tasks involved for the most common Subaru maintenance intervals.Prices vary by year, model and engine, so please contact us so that we may provide you with an accurate quote.

OIL AND FILTER SERVICE

    • Change engine oil, filter, and drain plug gasket
  • Top of washer fluid
  • Check engine coolant
  • Check brake wear, belts, and hoses
  • Check pressure in all tires
  • Check exhaust system and lights

6k MI / MINOR SERVICE

  • Change engine oil, filter, and drain plug gasket
  • Rotate tires
  • Top off all fluid levels
  • Check engine coolant
  • Check brake wear, belts, and hoses
  • Check exhaust system and lights
  • Check pressure in all tires

30K MI / 90k MI SERVICE

  • Change engine oil, filter, and drain plug gasket
  • Inspect and adjust drive belts
  • Inspect fuel lines
  • Replace air cleaner element
  • Replace cabin filter
  • Inspect clutch system (if applicable)
  • Inspect brake lines
  • Replace brake fluid and flush system
  • Inspect brake linings and drums
  • Inspect suspension components
  • Inspect wheel bearings, axle boots, and joints
  • Rotate tires
  • Change front and rear differential fluid (replace all drain/fill/check plugs gaskets)
  • Change transmission fluid (Optional on CVT Transmissions)
  • Add fuel system cleaner
  • Change spark plugs on SOHC EJ engines only
SCHEDULE AN APPOINTMENT

60k MI / 120K MI SERVICE

  • Replace engine oil, filter and drain plug washer
  • Inspect and adjust all drive belts to factory specifications
  • Inspect and ensure cooling fan is operating within factory specifications
  • Replace CVT transmission fluid with Subaru OEM fluid. Manual transmissions are every 30,000-mile interval. 2009 and older models are 30,000-mile intervals with TYPE HP synthetic ATF
  • Check to ensure air conditioning and heating systems are operating within factory specifications
  • Rotate tires, inspect tread wear and check and adjust tire pressure as needed
  • Perform brake system inspection; pads and/or drums, lines, hoses and fluid
  • Inspect suspension system and steering components to ensure factory specifications
  • Service battery, clean terminals, install anti-corrosion pads and check battery condition
  • Inspect wiper blades and linkage operation
  • Lubricate all door, trunk and hood latches and hinges if needed
  • Inspect exhaust system and heat shields
  • Replace front and rear differential fluid with full synthetic fluid
  • Inspect radiator and cooling system
  • Replace engine coolant and test protection levels 2008 and older is every 30,000-mile interval (2009 and newer interval is 137,250 and isn’t due until then. Blue Super Coolant)
  • Adjust emergency brake to within factory specifications as needed
  • Replace engine air filter element and or clean K&N style if equipped
  • Inspect steering operation, tie rod ends and steering rack guides per factory specifications
  • Inspect all engine and transmission mounts
  • Check all interior and exterior lighting operation
  • Replace/perform brake fluid exchange
  • Replace all spark plugs. 2010 and newer 60,000-mile intervals. 2009 and older 30,000-mile intervals. All 6 cylinders and turbo models are 60k intervals, all years
  • Install premium fuel additive into gas tank
  • Factory trained technician to conduct road test
  • Replace Pollen/Cabin air filter every 12,000 miles (if equipped)
  • Replace fuel filter in 2004 and older vehicles only every 30,000 miles
