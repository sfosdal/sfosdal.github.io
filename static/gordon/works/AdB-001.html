About the artist

Albert de Belleroche 1864–1944
Born in Swansea to a French noble family, trained in Paris, and a lifelong friend of John Singer Sargent, who painted him repeatedly. Best known as a lithographer. Father of William; the title passed to his son on his death in 1944. His work is out of copyright, which is why the images below are public domain. Wikipedia
