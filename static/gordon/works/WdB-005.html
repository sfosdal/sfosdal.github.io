About the artist

William de Belleroche 1913–1969
Self-taught painter, known as 'Count Willie'. Met Frank Brangwyn in 1934 and became his devotee, biographer and buyer, arranging Brangwyn's 1952 Royal Academy retrospective. Painted shells, crustacea and portraits of a wide Brighton and London circle. He has no Wikipedia article and almost no public image record — the gap this catalogue exists to fill.
