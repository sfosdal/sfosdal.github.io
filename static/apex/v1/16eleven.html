Good to know

Frequently asked

Is the restaurant 21+ only?

Yes. Guests must be 21 or older with valid ID to enter.

Where are you located?

We are inside APEX Everett, in downtown Everett, Washington — 1611 Everett Ave, Everett WA 98201, on the first level.

Do I need an event ticket to dine here?

No, you do not need a ticket to dine with us unless otherwise noted on specific event nights.

What are your hours?

Monday – Thursday: 4:00 PM – 9:00 PM
Friday & Saturday: 4:00 PM – 10:30 PM

What type of food do you serve?

Our menu features steak, seafood, elevated bar food, and shareable plates.

Do you have vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free options?

Yes, we offer a variety of options to accommodate dietary preferences and restrictions.
