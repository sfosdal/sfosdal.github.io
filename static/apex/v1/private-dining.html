The room

Refined, tailored, yours

An intimate and stylish space, 16Eleven's Private Dining Room is ideal for everything from corporate events and business dinners to birthday celebrations and special occasions. Personalized service and a chef-crafted menu featuring steakhouse classics and Japanese-inspired flavors make for a refined dining experience tailored to your needs.

Seats up to 40 guests for dinner, or up to 55 for a cocktail-style reception. For larger events, ask about the Gatsby Room or Kings Hall.

Guests must be 21 and over. A deposit is required to secure your date.
