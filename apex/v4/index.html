The restoration

Meticulously restored, deliberately alive.

Enter a world where history and modern creativity collide. APEX is more than a venue — it's a landmark restored brick by brick, then handed back to the city with music in the rafters and dinner on the stove.

This building was made for long nights out. Come make history with us.

Floor by floor

Three storeys, three acts.

Storey I

16Eleven

Named for the address it has kept for a century. Steak, seafood and shareable plates in the restored first-floor dining room — the culinary cornerstone of the building. The supper club, revived.
