A museum you drink in

The DogTown Collection isn't roped off in a white-walled gallery. It hangs over dinner at 16Eleven, watches the room at El Sid, and lines the walls of Kings Hall and the Gatsby Room — history and street culture sharing the same brick.

Come for a show, stay for the walls. The collection is on view whenever the building is open.
