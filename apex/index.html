The building

Step inside

Enter a world where history and modern creativity collide. Welcome to APEX Everett, a landmark destination in the heart of downtown, where every event transforms into an unforgettable experience.

More than just a venue, APEX is a journey. We've meticulously restored a historic building, weaving in the vibrant energy of art, music, and culinary excellence to offer a truly unique setting for your next event.

the DogTown Collection

The world's largest collection of graffiti and urban art lives on these three floors — over dinner, behind the bar, and around the stage. Every visit is a viewing.

16Eleven · First floor

Dine

16Eleven seamlessly blends historic charm with modern flair, perfectly complementing APEX Everett's fusion of old and new. Located within a beautifully preserved historical Everett building, it serves as the culinary cornerstone for the venue's museum, cocktail lounge, and Kings Hall events.

Enjoy 16Eleven as a standalone experience, or use it as your starting point to explore all that APEX Everett has to offer.

Explore 16Eleven

El Sid & Kings Hall · Upstairs

Turn it up

Dinner is but the opening act at APEX Everett. Upstairs at El Sid, cozy booths and low pendant lighting set the scene. This is where you'll find sophisticated sips and snacks with a punk rock edge.

One floor higher, Kings Hall will take your night through the roof with the finest live music venue that the region has to offer.

Pick your floor, or pick all three. This building was made for long nights out – come make history with us.
