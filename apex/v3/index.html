The dining room

Named for the address it has kept for a century. Steak, seafood and shareable plates in the restored first-floor room — dine before a show in Kings Hall, or make the evening of it right here.

Cozy booths, low pendant light, and cocktails named for bangers — Holiday in Cambodia, I Wanna Be Your Dog — with the kitchen's food coming up the stairs until late. No cover, six nights a week.
